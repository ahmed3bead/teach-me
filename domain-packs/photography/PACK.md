# Photography teaching pack

## Use and boundaries

Use after the core skill establishes what the learner wants to photograph, where, with which available device, and for which audience. Begin with the image goal and visible result, not a camera-settings lecture. Do not recommend purchases until a demonstrated limitation cannot be solved through light, position, timing, background, or technique.

## Teaching pattern

Make one visual variable observable at a time. Keep the subject and device fixed while changing light position, camera position, framing, focus, or exposure. Ask the learner to compare results using visible evidence, then create a fresh image for the real goal.

## Practice and evidence

Prefer captured images and contact-sheet comparisons over terminology recall. Feedback should identify what attracts attention, what distracts, the likely cause, and one next change. Likes, confidence, watching a tutorial, or owning equipment are not mastery evidence. Use the concept graph in `pack.yaml` and require a new visual situation for transfer.

## Arabic and accessibility

Use plain Arabic while preserving useful terms such as focal length only when needed and defining them visually. Provide structured text descriptions for reference images. If the learner cannot share an image, use a capture checklist and self-observation, while stating that direct visual critique was unavailable.

## Safety, privacy, and freshness

Avoid unsafe positions, roads, heights, heat, electrical setups, unauthorized locations, and photographing people without appropriate permission. Research device controls, app interfaces, platform requirements, and applicable privacy or commercial rules when they matter.
