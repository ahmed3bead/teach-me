# Domain pack roadmap

This is a hypothesis-driven launch order, not a permanent ranking. Reassess it with consented usage data, community requests, search demand, maintainability, and access to qualified reviewers.

## Recommended first wave

1. **AI literacy and practical AI use** — broad current demand and a strong fit for an AI-native teacher.
2. **English communication** — large global audience and a clear Arabic–English advantage.
3. **Programming and web development** — highly testable through executable learner work.
4. **Data, Excel, and analytics** — practical workplace outcomes and objective exercises.
5. **Design and UX fundamentals** — useful across technical and creative careers; requires visual critique support.

## Second wave

6. Digital marketing and content creation.
7. Photography and video production.
8. Project management and business communication.
9. Cybersecurity fundamentals.
10. Mathematics and quantitative reasoning.

## Selection score

Before building a pack, score it on:

- learner demand and underserved Arabic demand;
- ability to demonstrate improvement;
- availability of authoritative sources;
- access to a qualified reviewer;
- maintenance burden and rate of change;
- safety risk;
- suitability for Arabic and English;
- reuse across professions and countries.

Do not start all packs together. Build one pack, define its eval baseline, test it with real learners, improve the pack contract, and only then reuse that pattern.

## Initial evidence

The first-wave hypothesis is informed by the World Economic Forum's *Future of Jobs Report 2025* and current Coursera topic and career-program groupings, which emphasize AI, data, cybersecurity, software, communication, project management, UX, and marketing. These sources indicate broad market direction; they do not prove demand for this specific product.
