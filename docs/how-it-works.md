# How Teach Me works

Teach Me is designed around what the learner can eventually do, not around the amount of text produced.

## The teaching loop

1. **Establish the goal and starting point.** Ask only questions that can change where teaching begins.
2. **Choose one useful next objective.** Keep the scope and cognitive load manageable.
3. **Explain and demonstrate.** Use a suitable representation and a relevant example.
4. **Adapt to confusion.** Change representation, pace, terminology, or prerequisite instead of repeating the same explanation.
5. **Check with consent.** Offer routine assessment only after a meaningful learning unit.
6. **Treat progress honestly.** Reading, confidence, or assisted practice alone does not prove independent ability or retention.

## Supported workflows

| Workflow | Use it for | What Teach Me does |
|---|---|---|
| Learner | Learning, practice, review, or getting unstuck | Teaches progressively and adapts to demonstrated needs |
| Educator | Preparing an authorized lesson, workshop, activity, or assessment | Preserves traceability and separates teacher-only details |
| Source-grounded | Learning from a book, document, accessible video, course, website, or curriculum | Records actual access, verifies important claims, and never presents inaccessible material as inspected |

The audience and input are independent. A learner can study a source, and an educator can prepare a lesson from one.

## Language

Teach Me supports:

- English (`en`).
- Simplified Modern Standard Arabic (`ar-MSA`).

Arabic dialect input receives simplified Modern Standard Arabic unless the learner explicitly requests English. Technical terms and proper names stay in their original script and are explained when needed.

## Evidence, privacy, and safety

- Current, disputed, consequential, or high-stakes claims require suitable authoritative evidence.
- Source access and coverage are stated honestly.
- Learner progress is recorded only when the host provides an authorized persistent workspace.
- Personal data, private transcripts, protected curricula, and credentials must not be exposed.
- Educational content does not replace qualified medical, legal, financial, or other high-stakes professional advice.
